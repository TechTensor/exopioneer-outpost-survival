# AstroBase – NASA Space Apps Challenge Submission Draft

## Project name
# ExoPioneer – Outpost Survival Simulator

## One-line summary
An interactive browser game that teaches students how astronauts must balance life support, power, food production and radiation protection to keep a lunar or Martian outpost operating.

## Problem / opportunity
Long-duration space exploration requires teams to make connected decisions about limited resources and environmental risks. These engineering trade-offs can be difficult for younger learners to understand through text alone.

## Our solution
AstroBase turns outpost planning into a short, playable simulation. Players select a lunar or Martian destination, manage six habitat systems and choose one major operation each day. Daily resource consumption and unexpected events demonstrate how decisions affect mission survival. Players must keep the crew safe for 30 simulated days.

## Key features
- Moon and Mars mission scenarios.
- Live resource dashboard for oxygen, water, food, power, radiation safety and habitat integrity.
- Strategic upgrades for solar power, greenhouse production, recycling, shielding and repairs.
- Random operational events and a mission activity log.
- Science score and mission outcome report.
- Runs in a modern browser without installation.

## How it addresses the challenge
The challenge asks for an interactive game or app that lets students run a lunar or Martian outpost and experience trade-offs involving life support, radiation shielding, power and food production. AstroBase directly models these systems as resources and gives the player decisions that affect whether the mission succeeds.

## Tools and technologies
HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The project uses browser-native functionality and does not require a backend or external API.

## Limitations
The resource values, event probabilities and system effects are simplified for educational playability. They are not real spacecraft specifications, NASA operational data or engineering recommendations.
