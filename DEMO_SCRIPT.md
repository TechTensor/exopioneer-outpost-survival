# 60–90 second demo video script

**0–10 sec – Introduction**  
“Hello, this is AstroBase, an interactive outpost survival simulator created for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer challenge.”

**10–20 sec – Select a mission**  
“Players can choose a lunar or Martian outpost. Each destination has different environmental challenges.”

**20–40 sec – Resource dashboard**  
“The dashboard tracks oxygen, water, food, power, radiation safety and habitat integrity. Every mission day, the crew consumes resources.”

**40–65 sec – Make decisions**  
“Players choose one operation per day. For example, deploying solar arrays improves power generation, while servicing recycling helps conserve water and oxygen. Each action has a cost, so the player must plan ahead.”

**65–80 sec – Events and outcome**  
“Unexpected events such as water leaks, power fluctuations and dust storms create additional challenges. The goal is to keep the habitat operational for 30 days.”

**80–90 sec – Closing**  
“By turning resource management into a game, AstroBase helps learners understand the connected decisions behind sustainable human exploration.”
